Frequently asked questions

How do I get back something I bought?

Open Settings (the gear at the top right) and tap Restore Purchases. This restores Ad-Free Boosts and the Starter Pack on any device signed in with the same Apple ID. Pearl packs are used up when they're delivered, so they can't be restored.

I bought something but didn't receive it.

Make sure you're online and restart the game; the store delivers pending purchases when the game opens. If it still doesn't arrive, email us with the date of the purchase. For refunds, Apple handles all payments: visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game?

Yes. Your ducks, Squeaks, feathers and collection are saved only on your device, so deleting the game or resetting your device removes them. Purchases are safe and can be restored as described above.

Why did my offline earnings stop growing?

Your ducks nap after a few hours away. The limit starts at 4 hours and grows with the Long Nap upgrade, the Deep Sleep feather perk and the Sleepy Duck from the Hatchery.

The "Watch Ad" button doesn't work.

Ads need an internet connection and are sometimes briefly unavailable. Wait a moment and try again. Some free rewards also have a daily limit that resets at midnight (UTC).

What does Pull the Plug do?

It drains the water: your Squeaks, ducks and upgrades reset, and you earn Golden Feathers that boost all production forever. Feathers, feather perks, Hatchery ducks, Pearls, Bread Crumbs and achievements are kept.

How do I start over?

Open Settings and tap Reset Progress twice. This can't be undone. Purchases can still be restored afterwards.
