Duck Army – Privacy Policy

Pond Pixel · Effective date: October 9, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Duck Army ("the Game") uses, why, and the choices you have. The Game is developed by Pond Pixel ("we", "us").

The short version: we don't ask for your name, email or any account. Your game progress is saved only on your device. To keep the Game free, it shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use device information as described below. Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (for example Squeaks, ducks, upgrades, Pearls, achievements) and your settings (sound, music, number format) are saved locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. It is removed if you delete the Game or use Settings → Reset Progress (one-time purchases can be restored with Restore Purchases).

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, mainly optional "rewarded" ads that you choose to watch in exchange for in-game bonuses. To deliver, measure and protect these ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking (see below);
  • device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interactions (for example which ads were shown or tapped).

Google's use of this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

3. Your choices about tracking

  • App Tracking Transparency (iPhone/iPad): the Game asks for your permission before your advertising identifier can be used for personalized ads. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device.
  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: before ads load, the Game shows a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform where you can accept or decline personalized ads.
  • Turning off tracking or declining consent does not limit any features of the Game.

4. In-app purchases

Optional purchases (such as Ad-Free Boosts, the Starter Pack or Pearl packs) are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. We only learn which product was bought, so the Game can deliver it. Apple's handling of your data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy (apple.com/legal/privacy).

5. Unity

The Game is built with the Unity engine and uses Unity services to process in-app purchases. Unity may collect limited technical information (for example device type, operating system, crash and performance diagnostics) to operate and improve its services, as described in Unity's Privacy Policy (unity.com/legal/privacy-policy).

6. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Because we do not keep personal information about you ourselves, most requests are handled through the choices above or directly with Google, Apple or Unity. You are welcome to contact us with any request or question.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the Game gains new features. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the effective date at the top.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or the Game: pondpixel.games@outlook.com
